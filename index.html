01 · Research foundation

From fragmented supervision to one transparent workflow

IPMS addresses the disconnected planning, monitoring, assessment, and administrative work surrounding undergraduate software projects.

The challenge

Supervisors see reports, meetings, repositories, and schedules—but rarely as one connected story.

Manual processes make it difficult to form balanced teams, identify proposal weaknesses, distribute work fairly, detect delivery risk early, and verify whether reported progress is supported by evidence.

01

Skill and preference conflicts during team formation

02

Subjective planning and uneven individual workload

03

Late risk detection and incomplete contribution visibility

04

Slow, inconsistent proposal feedback and mentorship

Research objective
◎

Build an intelligent, human-governed project management system for undergraduate research.

The platform combines multi-objective optimization, machine learning, NLP, scheduling algorithms, and auditable evidence while leaving academic decisions with authorized staff.
